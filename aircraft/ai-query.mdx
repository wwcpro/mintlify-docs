## How it works

The AI search bar (marked with a ✦ sparkle icon) uses Claude AI to convert your plain-English description into a database query. You don't need to know column names, filter syntax, or SQL.

Just describe what you're looking for.

## Writing good queries

The AI understands aviation terminology, aircraft categories, and location context.

### By type

```
all jets for sale
turboprops in my territory
light jets under 10 years old
helicopters based at my airports
```

### By manufacturer or model

```
Cessnas for sale
G650s
Phenom 300s
King Air 350s
```

### By location

```
jets based at ARR
aircraft at KARR
turboprops in Texas
```

<Note>
  You can use 3-letter IATA codes (ARR) or 4-letter ICAO codes (KARR) — both work.
</Note>

### By sale status or price

```
heavy jets for sale
aircraft listed under 5 million
jets with asking price
```

### Combined queries

```
Cessnas for sale in my territory
mid-size jets delivered after 2018
turboprops based at ARR for sale
```

## Example queries and what they find

| Query | What it searches |
|-------|-----------------|
| `all jets for sale` | All aircraft where category includes "Jet" AND listed for sale |
| `G650s in Texas` | Gulfstream G650s based in Texas airports |
| `heavy jets under 5M` | Large/Ultra-Long-Range jets with asking price info |
| `turboprops based at ARR` | Turboprops at Aurora Municipal Airport |
| `Cessnas for sale` | Any Cessna listed for sale in your territory |

## Tip chips (example searches)

The "Try AI Search" box below the search bar shows example queries tailored to your territory — the state and airport codes shown are from **your** franchise territory, not generic examples.

Click any chip to populate the search bar, then press Search.

## Search results

Results show up to 50 aircraft per page. If there are more, a **Load more** button appears at the bottom.

Each result card shows:
- Tail number
- Make, model, year
- Base airport code
- Owner company / contact
- For Sale badge

Click any card to open the full aircraft profile.

## What if I get no results?

Try broadening your search:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Too specific a query">
    Instead of `2020 Cessna Citation CJ3+ for sale`, try `Citation CJ3 for sale`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Spelling or model name variation">
    Try the make alone: `Cessnas` or the ICAO model code: `C525`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Territory too narrow">
    Your territory may not have that aircraft type. Try removing location context from your query.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
