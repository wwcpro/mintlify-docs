---
title: Aircraft Photos
description: JetNet photos on every aircraft, plus your own photos once it belongs to a customer.
---

Open any aircraft and click the **Photos** tab. The number on the tab is how many photos there are.

## JetNet photos

- Photos load from JetNet when you open the aircraft, labeled by type (Exterior, Cockpit, Interior, Galley) and year.
- The first look at a tail takes a few seconds while the photos are fetched. After that they load right away.
- They're checked against JetNet once a week. **Refresh** checks again now.
- Some aircraft have no JetNet photos; the tab says so.

## Our photos

Once the aircraft belongs to one of your customers, **Our photos** lets your team add its own pictures, like before and after shots, alongside JetNet's.
