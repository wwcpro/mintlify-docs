## What is the Airports tab?

The **Airports** tab (next to Aircraft on the Aircraft Data page) shows every airport in your franchise territory on an interactive map, plus a card grid below it.

Each airport card shows:
- IATA/ICAO code
- Airport name
- City and state
- Region

Click any airport card to open its detail page.

## Airport detail page

The airport detail page gives you a full picture of activity at that location:

- **Aircraft Based Here** — all aircraft with that airport as their home base, pulled from JetNet
- **Departures** — recent outbound flight activity
- **Arrivals** — recent inbound flight activity

<Tip>
  Click any aircraft in the "Based Here" list to jump straight to that aircraft's full profile.
</Tip>

## Territory filtering

Just like Aircraft search, Airports are automatically filtered to your franchise territory. You only see airports assigned to your account — you won't see airports outside your territory.

Admins see all airports platform-wide with no restrictions.

## Finding a specific airport

Use the card grid to browse alphabetically, or look for the airport code or city name. For large territories (e.g. New Jersey with 90+ airports), scroll through the grid or use your browser's find-in-page.
