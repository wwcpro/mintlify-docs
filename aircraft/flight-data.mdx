## What is Flight Data?

The platform pulls real-time flight activity from JetNet for every airport and individual aircraft in your territory. This lets you see which aircraft are actively flying — a strong signal for service outreach timing.

## Where to find it

Flight data appears in two places:

### On an Airport detail page
Navigate to **Aircraft Data → Airports → [Airport name]**.

You'll see two sections:
- **Departures** — recent flights originating from this airport
- **Arrivals** — recent flights landing at this airport

Each flight record shows tail number, aircraft type, origin/destination, date, and time.

### On an Aircraft detail page
Navigate to **Aircraft Data → [Aircraft]** and click the **Recent Flights** tab.

This shows the full flight history for that specific aircraft — not filtered by airport.

## How current is the data?

Flight data is cached for **24 hours** per aircraft. The first time you load a record, it may take a few seconds to fetch from JetNet. Subsequent loads within 24 hours are instant.

<Note>
  Not all aircraft transmit ADS-B or file flight plans. Some aircraft will show limited or no flight history even if they are actively flying.
</Note>

## Using flight data for outreach

Active flight patterns are one of the best indicators that an owner is engaged with their aircraft. Consider reaching out when:

- An aircraft shows **recent departure activity** — the owner is actively using it
- An aircraft **arrives at an airport in your territory** — a potential new contact in your area
- An aircraft has **no recent activity** — the owner may be looking to sell or reduce costs

<Tip>
  Save a list of aircraft at a specific airport, then check back regularly on their flight activity to identify high-value outreach moments.
</Tip>

## JetNet data coverage

Flight data comes from JetNet, which aggregates FAA records, ADS-B feeds, and operator filings. Coverage is strongest for **Part 91 and Part 135 business aviation** aircraft. General aviation piston aircraft and international-only operations may have limited history.

We are actively working to expand coverage for aircraft not currently tracked by JetNet.
