## Your Territory

As a franchisee, your Aircraft Data view is automatically scoped to your territory airports. When you search, the results only show aircraft based at airports in your territory — you don't need to add location filters manually.

<Note>
  Admins see all aircraft platform-wide with no territory restrictions.
</Note>
