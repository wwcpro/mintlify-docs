## What is the CRM?

The Real Clean Aircraft CRM is your sales pipeline — purpose-built for aircraft detailing franchisees. Every prospect you work has a record here with full contact info, activity history, linked aircraft, quotes, and tasks.

## Pipeline Stages

Every prospect moves through these stages:

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **New** | Just added — not yet contacted |
| **Contacted** | First outreach made |
| **Qualified** | Confirmed interest + fit |
| **Quoted** | Proposal sent |
| **Negotiating** | Active back-and-forth |
| **Won** | Deal closed, promote to Customer |
| **Lost** | Not moving forward (record reason) |

<Tip>
  Drag cards between columns on the board, or click the stage badge on any prospect to change it with a dropdown. Both are instant — no page reload.
</Tip>

## Navigation

Find the CRM in the left sidebar under **CRM → Prospects**. The CRM group contains:

- **Prospects** — your pipeline
- **Customers** — converted accounts
- **Companies** — linked organizations
- **Quotes** — all quotes across prospects

## Two Views

The prospect list has two layouts — toggle between them with the grid/list icons at the top right of the board:

| View | Best for |
|------|----------|
| **Board** | Seeing the whole pipeline at a glance, dragging between stages |
| **List** | Scanning many prospects, bulk selecting, sorting |
