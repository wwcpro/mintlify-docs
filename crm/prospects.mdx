## The Board

The Prospects board is your main pipeline view at **CRM → Prospects**.

### Board view

Prospects are organized into columns by stage. Each card shows:

- Contact name and company
- Home airport and aircraft type
- Tags (up to 3 shown)
- Last updated date
- Franchise avatar (if multi-franchise account)

**Drag a card** to a different column to change its stage instantly.

**Click a card** to open the full prospect detail.

### List view

Click the list icon (top right) to switch to a compact table with all prospects in a single scrollable list. The list view adds:

- Inline checkbox selection for bulk actions
- Clickable tags (click a tag to instantly filter by it)
- Inline "+ tag" button on each row
- Source and stage columns

## Sorting and counts

The page header shows total prospect count. Prospects are sorted by most recently updated by default.

## Adding prospects

Use the **+ New Prospect** button in the top right to open the [add prospect form](/crm/add-prospect).

Use the **Import** button next to it to [bulk import from a CSV file](/crm/import).

## Do Not Contact (DNC)

Prospects marked DNC show a red ban icon on their card. They are automatically excluded from bulk email campaigns. See the [DNC section](/crm/prospect-detail#do-not-contact) for how to mark/unmark a prospect.
