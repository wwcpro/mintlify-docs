## Search bar

The search bar at the top of the Prospects board searches across:
- Contact name
- Company name
- Email address
- Home airport code
- Aircraft type

Results filter in real time as you type — no submit required. The count next to "CRM / Prospects" updates to show how many prospects match.

## Filter bar

Click the **Filters** button (or the filter icon) to expand the full filter bar. Available filters:

### Stage filter

Click any stage chip to show only prospects in that stage:

`New` `Contacted` `Qualified` `Quoted` `Negotiating` `Won` `Lost`

Click the active chip again to clear it.

### Tag filter

A dropdown lists all tags across your prospects. Select one to show only prospects with that tag. Tags can also be clicked directly in list view rows to filter.

### Source filter

Filter by how the prospect entered your CRM:
- JetNet (created from aircraft search)
- Manual (added by hand)
- CSV import
- Inbound form

### DND toggle

Turn on the **DNC** toggle to show only Do Not Contact prospects. Useful for auditing your suppression list.

## Active filter pills

When the filter bar is collapsed, any active filters appear as small pills directly below the search bar. Each pill shows the filter name and an × to remove it individually.

## Clearing filters

Click **Clear** in the filter bar to reset all filters at once, or click the × on individual filter pills.

<Tip>
  Combine search + filters. For example: search "Citation" + filter stage "Quoted" to quickly find all Citation prospects in your quote stage.
</Tip>
