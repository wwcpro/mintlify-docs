## Why link aircraft?

Knowing which aircraft a prospect owns or operates is core to your pitch. It tells you the service scope, the cleaning complexity, and the revenue potential before you ever get on a call.

The Linked Aircraft panel on a prospect record shows all aircraft you've associated with that contact.

## Linking an aircraft

From any prospect detail page, find the **Linked Aircraft** panel in the center column.

Click **+ Add Aircraft** to open the lookup modal.

### JetNet lookup (recommended)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the tail number">
    Type the registration number (e.g. `N12345`) and click **Search**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the match">
    If found in JetNet, you'll see:
    - Tail number
    - Make, model, and year
    - Category/size (Light Jet, Multi-Engine Turboprop, etc.)
    - Home base airport

    Click **Link This Aircraft** to associate it with the prospect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmed">
    The aircraft card appears immediately in the panel. For JetNet aircraft, a **View** link takes you to the full aircraft record with flight history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Manual entry (not in JetNet)

Not all aircraft are in JetNet — particularly older piston aircraft, light experimentals, and some international registrations. If the tail number isn't found:

- The modal shows "Not found in JetNet — fill in details below"
- Enter make, model, year, and optional notes manually
- Click **Add Aircraft**

You can also click **Skip JetNet — add manually** at any time to go straight to the manual form without searching.

<Note>
  Manual aircraft are saved with a label showing "Manual entry" so it's clear the data wasn't verified against JetNet.
</Note>

## Viewing linked aircraft

Each aircraft card in the panel shows:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Tail number | Blue registration badge |
| Make / Model / Year | Combined as one line |
| Source label | "JetNet" or "Manual entry" |
| Notes | Any notes you added on link |
| View link | Opens full aircraft record (JetNet only) |

## Removing an aircraft

Hover any aircraft card and click the **trash icon** that appears. The aircraft is removed immediately.

## Multiple aircraft

A prospect can have any number of linked aircraft. This is common for operators and FBOs who manage fleets.
