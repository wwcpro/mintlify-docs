## Selecting prospects

Switch to **list view** to enable bulk selection.

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Select one | Click the checkbox on the left of any row |
| Select all visible | Click the checkbox in the header row |
| Deselect all | Click the header checkbox again |

The number of selected prospects appears in the floating action bar at the bottom of the screen.

## Available bulk actions

Once prospects are selected, click **Actions** in the bottom bar to see:

### Tag

Adds a tag to all selected prospects. Type the tag name and confirm. The tag is added without removing existing tags.

### Move to Stage

Changes the lead stage for all selected prospects at once. Choose the new stage from the dropdown.

### Mark DNC

Sets the Do Not Contact flag on all selected prospects. They'll be excluded from future campaigns.

### Email Campaign

Opens the bulk email modal:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Select an email template from your library. Templates are managed under **Communications → Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm recipients">
    The modal shows how many of the selected prospects have email addresses on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Pick a send time, or send immediately. The job is queued and processed within 5 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Prospects with DNC enabled are automatically excluded even if selected.
</Note>

### Delete

Permanently deletes all selected prospects after a confirmation prompt. This cannot be undone.

## Export CSV

The **Export CSV** button (bottom bar) downloads a spreadsheet of all selected prospects with all fields.

## Feedback

All bulk actions update the UI immediately. A toast notification confirms success or failure. If the action fails for any reason, the board reverts to its previous state.
