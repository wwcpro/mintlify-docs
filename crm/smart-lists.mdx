## What is a Smart List?

A Smart List is a saved combination of filters — stage, tag, source, DND, and search text. Once saved, you can reload it with one click instead of re-applying filters every time.

Examples:
- "Hot Turboprop Leads" → stage: Qualified, tag: Turboprop
- "Quoted This Month" → stage: Quoted
- "DNC Review" → DNC toggle on

## Saving a Smart List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply your filters">
    Use the search bar and filter bar to get the exact view you want to save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Smart Lists">
    Click the **Bookmark** icon (top right of the board, near the view toggle). A dropdown opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save current view">
    Click **Save current view**, type a name, and press Enter or click the checkmark.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list is saved to your franchise account and persists across sessions and devices.

## Loading a Smart List

Click the Bookmark icon and select any saved list from the dropdown. The board instantly applies those filters and updates the count.

## Deleting a Smart List

Open the Smart Lists dropdown and click the trash icon next to any list.

<Note>
  Smart Lists save the filter *criteria*, not specific prospect records. If new prospects match the criteria after you save the list, they'll appear when you load it.
</Note>
