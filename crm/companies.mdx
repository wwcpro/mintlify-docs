## What are companies?

Companies represent the organizations your prospects belong to — FBOs, charter operators, corporate flight departments, fleet managers, and so on.

Linking prospects to companies lets you:
- See all contacts at the same organization in one place
- Understand your total relationship with an account
- Track company-level activity across multiple contacts

## Viewing companies

Go to **CRM → Companies** in the sidebar.

Each company card shows:
- Company name
- City and state
- Phone and website (if on file)

Click a company to open its detail page.

## Company detail page

The company detail page shows:
- Contact info (phone, address, website)
- All prospects linked to this company
- Linked aircraft (aircraft associated with the company's prospects)

## Linking a prospect to a company

### When adding a new prospect

The [New Prospect form](/crm/add-prospect) has a Company field with autocomplete. Start typing — matching companies appear as a dropdown. Select one to link the prospect.

### On an existing prospect

Open the prospect detail and click **Edit Contact**. The Company field has the same autocomplete. Select from the dropdown to link to an existing company, or type a new name to set it as free text.

## The company card on a prospect

When a prospect is linked to a company, a **company card** appears in the prospect's left sidebar showing:
- Company name and location
- A chevron link to the full company record

The prospect's company name in the header also becomes a clickable link to the company record.

## JetNet company connections

Companies imported from JetNet (via the Create Prospects action) may have a JetNet company ID on file. This is used for data linking but doesn't affect normal CRM use.
