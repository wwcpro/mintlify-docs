---
title: Adding Your Staff
description: Invite employees to your franchise and choose what each person can do.
---

Franchise owners and Franchise Admins can add staff from the **Team** page. Each person gets their own login, scoped to your franchise and your territory airports.

<Note>
**Team** shows in the sidebar for franchise owners and Franchise Admins only. Operations Managers, Technicians and Corporate accounts don't see it. If your login has no Team link, ask your franchise owner, or RealClean Admin, to add the person.
</Note>

## Invite someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    Click **Team** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start an invite">
    In the **Invite Employee** panel, click **New Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    First name, last name and email address. Email is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role">
    Choose **Franchise Admin**, **Operations Manager** or **Technician**. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Invite**. They get a RealClean email with a link to set their password. The invite link also shows on screen with a **Copy** button, so you can text it to them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Franchise Admin** | Everything the owner can do, including adding and removing staff on the Team page. |
| **Operations Manager** | Runs the shop. Leads, customers, quotes, the Estimator, invoices, the schedule and work orders, Aircraft Data, Lists and SOPs. Can't manage the team. |
| **Technician** | The phone app only. Their assigned jobs, checklists, before and after photos, and SOPs. No web access to sales or pricing. |

Technicians need the RealClean mobile app. Send them [realcleanaircraft.io/get-app/ios](https://realcleanaircraft.io/get-app/ios) for iPhone or [realcleanaircraft.io/get-app/android](https://realcleanaircraft.io/get-app/android) for Android. Those links always point at the current build.

## Change a role later

Under **Active Members**, click the role next to the person's name and pick a new one. The change takes effect the next time they load a page.

## Edit or remove someone

- **Edit member** (pencil icon) updates their name, email and mobile number.
- **Remove from franchise** takes away their access to your franchise.

## Invites that haven't been accepted

Pending invites are listed under **Invites**.

- **Resend invite** sends a fresh email with the same link.
- **Cancel invite** withdraws it, and the link stops working.
