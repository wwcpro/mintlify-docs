---
title: The Company Page
description: The whole account on one page, including fleet, aircraft on file, RCA services and value.
---

Click a company name anywhere: a board card, a lead, or **Directory → Companies**.

## The numbers at the top

| Number | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Fleet** | The company's total active fleet from JetNet, or the number typed on **Edit** |
| **On file** | How many aircraft are on our leads and customers at this company |
| **RCA services** | Shows once the company is a customer: completed jobs, how many aircraft they covered, and what's scheduled or open |

## Value

| Field | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Won to date** | Approved and completed quotes for people at this company |
| **Last 12 months** | The same, for the past year |
| **Projected annual** | The value on the board card. Click the pencil to change it |

## The rest of the page

- **People** lists everyone we know at the company with their stage. **Add person** opens a new lead already linked to the company.
- **Recent quotes** and **Aircraft** on file.
- **Activity** is the account's timeline, and each entry says which person it's about.
- **Edit** changes the address, fleet size, annual value, services interested in and notes.
- **Partner agreements** sets agreed pricing for national partners. See [Partner Pricing](/corporate/partner-pricing).

## Finding companies

**Directory → Companies** opens on Corporate and network companies, the accounts the whole network shares. **Show every franchise's companies** widens the list to all of them. **New Company** adds one without a lead.
