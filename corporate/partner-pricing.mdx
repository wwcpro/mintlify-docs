---
title: Partner Pricing
description: Set agreed prices for a national partner once, and every franchisee quotes them, locked.
---

For national partners like GAP and TransMedics, Corporate sets the agreed prices once. Every franchisee then sees them, locked, in the Estimator and the quote builder.

<Note>
  Only Corporate and Admins can open Partner agreements.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the partner's company page">
    Click **Partner agreements**, then **New agreement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the agreement">
    | Field | Notes |
    |-------|-------|
    | **Name** | For example *GAP 2027 service pricing* |
    | **Status** | Starts as **Draft**. Franchisees only see an agreement while it's **Active** |
    | **Pricing** | An **Agreed price list**, or a **Percent off the book price** |
    | **Effective from / to** | Leave a date blank for no limit on that side |
    | **Notes** | Terms, contacts, anything the team should know |

    Click **Create agreement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load the price list">
    Paste the list, or click **Choose CSV file**. The columns are:

    ```csv
    Service,Aircraft model,Price,Labor hours
    Wet Wash,Gulfstream G650/650ER,2150,22
    Disinsection,All,325,
    ```

    Name services and models the way the Estimator lists them. Write `All` as the model for a price that covers every aircraft; a price for a specific model wins over it. **Template** downloads a sample, and **Names the import recognizes** lists every service and model name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Click **Preview**, check the lines, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it Active">
    Set the status to **Active**. Franchisees now see **Partner pricing** in the Estimator and the quote builder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What franchisees see

Choosing the partner under **Partner pricing** locks every service the agreement prices. Locked prices can't be edited. Services the agreement doesn't cover keep the book price.
