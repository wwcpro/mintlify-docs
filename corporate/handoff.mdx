---
title: Sending to a Franchisee
description: Hand a corporate account to the franchise that will service it.
---

When an account is ready for local service, hand it to the franchise that will do the work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move it to Sent to Franchisee">
    Drag the card to the **Sent to Franchisee** column, or choose **Sent to Franchisee** from the stage menu on the lead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the franchise">
    The franchise that covers the aircraft's home airport is listed first. Use **Find a franchise** to search by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note (optional)">
    Context, timing, who to call. This is the only note the franchise sees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send to franchise**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The franchise gets the lead in its **New** column and its Inbox, marked **From Corporate**, with the aircraft attached and a task for the franchise owner.
- Your lead stays on the corporate board in **Sent to Franchisee**, and your Corporate notes stay with you.
- A banner on each lead links to the other, so you can follow how it goes.
- Need it somewhere else? Use **Send to another franchise** on the lead.
