---
title: The Corporate Board
description: One card per company, the ten corporate stages, and estimated values.
---

Open **Sales → Leads** while you're in the Corporate role. The tabs at the top switch between the **Corporate board**, the **Affiliate board** and **All franchises**.

## Cards are companies

Each card shows the company name large and the people you're working there underneath. A company with nobody on it yet says *No contact yet*.

- **Drag a card** to another column and everyone on it moves together.
- Click **+ Value** to enter the account's estimated annual value. Type it short, like `250K` or `1.3M`. It shows on the card and rolls up into the dashboard.
- **Tags** on any of the account's leads show on the card (up to three). Click one to filter the board to those accounts.
- Click the company name to open the [company page](/corporate/company-page), or a person to open their lead.

## The ten stages

Corporate uses its own stages. Franchise boards keep their seven.

| Stage | Use it for |
|-------|-----------|
| **New** | Just added |
| **Contacted** | First conversation has happened |
| **Qualifying** | Working out fit, fleet and decision makers |
| **RFP/RFQ** | They've asked for a proposal or quote |
| **Negotiating** | Terms in discussion |
| **Proposal Sent** | Waiting on their answer |
| **Won** | Signed |
| **Lost (freezer)** | Not now, worth revisiting |
| **Dead** | Not worth pursuing |
| **Sent to Franchisee** | Handed to the franchise that will service it. See [Sending to a Franchisee](/corporate/handoff) |

## Adding to a stage

**Add company or lead** (top right) starts a new account in New. The **+** at the top of any column starts it in that column's stage instead.

## Board and list

Switch between **Board** and **List** at the top right. The list view is best for bulk work: tick rows to tag them or move them to a stage together. **Filters** narrows the board, and **Smart Lists** saves a filter you use often.

## The other boards

| Board | What's on it |
|-------|--------------|
| **Corporate board** | National accounts |
| **Affiliate board** | The original RealClean operation around Chicago. Website and affiliate leads no franchise covers land here |
| **All franchises** | Every franchise's leads in one view |
