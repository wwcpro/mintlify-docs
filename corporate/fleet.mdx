---
title: Fleet & Frequency
description: Attach a company's active JetNet fleet to a lead, choose or remove aircraft, and set how often each one is serviced.
---

When a lead's company is linked to JetNet, the **Linked Aircraft** panel on the lead starts with a JetNet strip: *"Western Air Charter, Inc. has 27 active aircraft in JetNet, 21 not on this lead."*

## Attaching aircraft

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Attach the fleet** / **Attach all** | Adds every active aircraft that isn't on the lead, except ones someone removed |
| **Choose aircraft** | Opens a checklist: tail, year, model, base, and how JetNet ties it to the company. Tick the ones you want (or **Select all**) and click **Attach** |

*Active* means the aircraft is in operation, not deregistered, and the company owns, operates, leases, manages, charters, or holds the certificate for it. Brokers and previous owners aren't counted.

<Tip>
  A lead holds up to **250 aircraft**. Big operators run hundreds (NetJets has 766), so attaching starts with US tails, newest first. When a lead is full, the strip says so.
</Tip>

## Removing aircraft

Use the trash icon on an aircraft's row. It stays off: **Attach all** won't bring it back. If you change your mind, it's still in **Choose aircraft**, marked **removed earlier**. Tick it to bring it back.

## What each aircraft shows

- Tail, year and model
- How JetNet ties it to the company (for example *Certificate Holder* or *Charter Company*)
- JetNet base airport and city, and the owner
- **For sale** when JetNet lists it for sale
- **Quote**, which starts a quote with that aircraft filled in

## Service frequency

Pick a frequency on each aircraft: Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, Every 6 weeks, Every 2 months, Quarterly, Twice a year, Once a year, or As needed.

The lead adds them up above the list: **"About 78 services a year at the frequencies set."** That's the number to bring to a fleet conversation.
