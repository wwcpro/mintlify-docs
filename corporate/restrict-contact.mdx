---
title: Restricting a Contact
description: Keep franchisees from reaching out to a person Corporate manages.
---

Some relationships belong to Corporate. Restricting a person keeps franchisees from contacting them on their own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the lead">
    The banner at the top reads *Corporate can stop franchisees contacting this person.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Restrict contact">
    Add a reason if you like. Only Corporate and Admins see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Restrict contact**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What franchisees see

- The person still appears, so nobody adds them again.
- Their email and phone are hidden, and they can't be emailed.
- The page tells the franchisee to talk to Corporate.

The restriction covers every record for that person, including quote and invoice recipients and their customer login. **Lift restriction** on the same banner puts everything back.

<Tip>
  **Restrict contact** keeps franchisees away while Corporate still works the relationship. **Do Not Contact** (on the lead's left column) keeps the person out of all bulk email for everyone.
</Tip>
