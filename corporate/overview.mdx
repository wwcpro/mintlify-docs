title: Corporate Overview
description: How the corporate board works for national accounts, and where everything lives.
---

The corporate board is where the RealClean corporate team works **national accounts**. It works like a franchise's lead board with one big difference: **each card is a company**, not a person. The people you're talking to at that company sit on its card, and the aircraft JetNet ties to the company come with it.

<Note>
  The corporate board holds national accounts only. Website and affiliate leads never land here. They go to the franchise that covers the airport, or to the **Affiliate** board when no franchise covers it.
</Note>

## Getting there

Sign in at **realcleanaircraft.io**. Corporate team members open in the **Corporate** role. The yellow chip under your name shows the role you're working in; click it to switch if your account has more than one.

## The Corporate dashboard

| Tile | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Open accounts** | Companies on the board that aren't won, lost or dead |
| **Open pipeline** | The total of the estimated values on your cards |
| **Won** | Accounts in the Won column |
| **Sent to franchisees** | Leads handed to a franchise |
| **Your open tasks** | Tasks assigned to you |

Below the tiles, **Pipeline by stage** shows the count and value in every stage, and **Top open accounts** lists your biggest accounts once values are entered.
