## Who sees a note

Every note composer has a picker:

| Choice | Who can read it |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Me** | You, plus anyone above you (Admins). The default for a new note |
| **Corporate** | The corporate team and Admins. **Franchisees never see it** |
| **Entire Network** | Everyone who can see this lead, franchisees included |

The composer remembers your last choice, except **Entire Network**, which you choose on each note. Only the person who wrote a note, or an Admin, can change who sees it later.

<Note>
  When a lead is sent to a franchisee, your **Me** and **Corporate** notes stay with Corporate. The franchise starts with only what you write in the hand-off note.
</Note>

## @mentions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type @ in a note">
    Start typing a name and pick the person from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They're notified">
    They get a push notification, and the note shows in their **Inbox** under Mentions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can only mention someone who can see the note. Mentioning a franchisee in a Corporate note is refused, so nothing private leaks.

## Tasks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a task">
    On the lead, open the **Tasks** tab and click **Add Task**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Give it a title, a due date and time, and choose who it's **assigned to**. They get a push notification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it">
    The person can **Start** or **Decline** it. A declined task shows in your Inbox, where you can **Take it back** or **Reassign** it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Sales → Tasks** lists your tasks (**Mine**) and the team's (**Everyone**). Overdue tasks are also in the daily summary email.

## The Inbox

The **Inbox** collects your mentions, tasks handed to you, service requests, and any new inbound lead on the corporate board in one queue. Work it to zero.
