---
title: Adding a Company
description: Add a national account from JetNet with its details, people, lead type and fleet.
---

Click **Add company or lead** on the board or the Corporate dashboard. On the corporate board the **company comes first**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for the company">
    Start typing the name. Companies already in RealClean are listed first, then **From JetNet**, with each company's city and aircraft count.

    <Tip>
      The aircraft count in the search list is a quick count of what JetNet lists the company as owner or operator of. Once you pick the company you see its full **active fleet**, which also counts aircraft it manages, charters or holds the certificate for. They're both right; they count different things.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick it, or add a new one">
    A JetNet company comes in filled out: address, phone, website and fleet size. If we already have that company, you're linked to ours instead of getting a duplicate.

    Not in either list? Choose **Add "name" as a new company** and fill in the details yourself.

    Adding a second person at a company that's already on the board? Pick it here and they join the same card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the company details">
    A strip shows what JetNet has: the active fleet, how many people it lists, and the kind of business. Under **Company details**:

    | Field | Notes |
    |-------|-------|
    | **Fleet size** | Filled in from JetNet's active fleet. Change it if you know better |
    | **Annual value** | What the account is worth in a year. Type it short (`250K`, `1.3M`). This is the value on the board card |
    | **Services interested in** | Pick from the service list, or type your own |
    | **Corporate office address** | Filled in from JetNet when it has one |
    | **Company notes** | Anything the team should know |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach the fleet, or not">
    The box reads **"Attach the N active aircraft to this lead."** It's ticked for a company's first lead and unticked when the company already has a lead, so a second person at the same account doesn't carry the whole fleet again. You can always attach or choose aircraft later from the lead. See [Fleet & Frequency](/corporate/fleet).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the people">
    **People at the company in JetNet** lists everyone JetNet has, with titles, emails and phone numbers where it has them. Tick each person you're talking to and set their role. **Each one becomes a lead on the company's card.**

    Someone who isn't in JetNet? Fill in **Contact at this company** by hand. A contact is optional: leave it empty and the company goes on the board alone until you know who to call.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the lead type">
    Charter, Corporate, Local Maintenance Manager, MRO, Dispatch or FBO. Need another? **+ Add a type…** adds it to the list for the whole corporate team.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Optionally add more aircraft by tail number, a stage, a lead source, a location and notes, then click **Add to board**. You land on the new lead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The phone app's Add Lead also puts the company first, but stays quick on purpose: JetNet search, the people list and attaching the fleet are on the web. Open the lead on the web later to attach the fleet.
</Note>
