## Your first aircraft search

Follow these steps to run your first query and export the results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Aircraft Data">
    In the left sidebar, click **Aircraft Data** under the Platform section.

    You'll see the AI search bar and your territory airports listed in the top-right corner.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Type a natural language query">
    Click the search bar and type a plain-English description of the aircraft you're looking for.

    ```
    all jets for sale
    ```

    ```
    turboprops based at my airports
    ```

    ```
    Cessnas under 10 years old
    ```

    Press **Search** or hit Enter.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review your results">
    Results load instantly. Each card shows:
    - Tail number (registration)
    - Make, model, year
    - Base airport
    - Owner / operator
    - For Sale badge (if listed)

    Click any card to open the full aircraft detail page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export to CSV">
    Once results are showing, click the **⚡ lightning bolt** button in the bottom-right corner of the page.

    Select **Download CSV**. The file downloads immediately — no waiting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
