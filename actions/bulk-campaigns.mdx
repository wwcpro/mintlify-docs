title: Bulk Email Campaigns
description: Send an email campaign to aircraft owners from your search results.
---

## Overview

After a search, you can launch an email campaign directly from the Action Panel — no need to manually build a recipient list.

## How to use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search and refine">
    Run your aircraft search. The more targeted your query, the better your campaign relevance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Bulk Actions in the panel">
    Open ⚡ and click **Bulk Actions**. This takes you to the New Campaign page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an email template">
    Choose from your active email templates. Templates are managed under **Communications → Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose recipients">
    Select the recipient type:
    - **All Prospects** — your CRM prospects
    - **Aircraft Owners** — owners from JetNet
    - **Customers** — registered customers
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the send">
    Set a date and time. Campaigns process every 5 minutes after the scheduled time.

    Click **Schedule Campaign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Contacts with **Do Not Contact** (DNC) enabled are automatically excluded. The frequency cap also prevents sending more than 1 marketing email per recipient per 7 days.
</Note>
