## The Action Panel

After any AI search returns results, click the **⚡ lightning bolt** in the bottom-right corner to open the Action Panel.

The panel has five actions:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Refine Results** | Narrow down with a second query |
| **Download CSV** | Export full dataset to a spreadsheet |
| **Save Search** | Bookmark exact aircraft IDs as a Saved List |
| **Create Prospects** | Add aircraft owners to your CRM automatically |
| **Bulk Actions** | Launch an email campaign to the owners |

<Tip>
  The most powerful workflow: **Search → Refine → Create Prospects → Launch Campaign**. This takes a broad market query all the way to an email send in about 5 minutes.
</Tip>
